Apes vs Aliens: Free Cat Runner Game

How to play

Apes vs Aliens: drag or swipe left and right (or use the arrow keys or A/D) to lead your apes through the gates. Pick the gate that gives the bigger crowd. Your apes throw yarn at boxes and mice. At the end of each level the LAO Mega pounces (in the final castle it is LAO Mega Fluffington, a giant fluffy cat: hit her hard while she naps): move away from the red paw mark. Pass gates to fill the Purr Meter, then tap BIG APE (or press Space) to bonk the boss. Earn fish treats and spend them in the Cat Tree. Earn up to 3 paw-stars per level: clear it, reach the kitten goal, and dodge every pounce. There is no real money: the Cat Tree is where you spend the fish treats you earn.

Questions

Is Apes vs Aliens free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no real money. You earn fish treats by playing and spend them on upgrades in the Cat Tree.

Does Apes vs Aliens have ads?

No. This version has no ads.

How do I play?

Drag on the screen, or use the arrow keys or A/D, to steer your apes through the best gates. Plus and multiply gates grow your parade; minus and divide gates shrink it.

How many levels are there?

There are 8 levels, each ending in a boss fight, plus a 60-second survival mode.

Do I need to download anything or sign up?

No. It runs in your browser and progress is saved on your device.

Who are the bosses?

A sneaky alien boss, and in the final castle LAO Mega Fluffington, a giant fluffy cat. Dodge the red paw when they pounce.
